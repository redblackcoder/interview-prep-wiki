# Restaurant reservations

Find and reserve a table at one restaurant without double-booking it.

## Recorded approach

Model tables and reservations, query for capacity and conflicts, then reserve a table. The board identifies the double-booking race and proposes a per-table slot bitmask. The free-table query and variable-length slots remained unresolved.
